## A note on what The Forge isn't

The Forge is a **workout tracking tool**, not medical advice. Periodization protocols, suggested weights, and rep targets are general fitness recommendations — not personalized medical or rehabilitation guidance. If you're recovering from an injury, managing a medical condition, or unsure whether a program suits you, consult a qualified coach or physician before training.

## Quick answers

### Account & login

**I forgot my password.**
On the login screen, tap "Forgot password" and we'll email a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive in 5 minutes.

**Can I change my email?**
Settings → Account → Change Email. You'll need to confirm via a link sent to the new address.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Account → Delete Account. This is permanent: training data, progress photos, and your push token are removed from our servers. Apple Health data on your phone is unaffected.

### Workouts

**The rest timer didn't start after my set.**
The rest timer fires when you save a set. If it didn't, force-quit and reopen the app — and email us with the date/time so we can dig into it.

**A PR didn't get flagged.**
PRs are calculated per exercise on best-set basis. If you swapped exercise variants (e.g. low-bar squat vs high-bar squat) the PR may be tracked against the variant you originally logged. Settings → Exercises lets you merge variants.

**My workout disappeared from the dashboard.**
Sessions older than the current week are archived to keep the Today view focused. They're still in Progress → History. Use the date picker to find them.

### Programs

**How do I switch programs mid-week?**
Programs tab → tap the program → "Make Active". The new program takes effect on next Monday's session. The current week's logged sessions stay attached to the old program.

**Can I edit The Forge program?**
Yes — duplicate it first (Programs → Forge → Duplicate), then edit the copy. The original stays untouched so you can always reset.

### Partner mode

**My partner isn't getting push notifications when I finish a session.**
Three things to check, in order:
1. They've enabled notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → The Forge.
2. They've connected with you (Partner tab → invite code).
3. They share the same program. Partner pushes only fire on shared programs.

If all three are true and pushes still don't arrive, email us.

**I want to disconnect my partner.**
Partner tab → tap partner name → Disconnect. They'll stop seeing your workouts immediately and you'll stop seeing theirs.

---

### Apple Health (iOS only)

**Health sync isn't working.**
Permissions are revocable by Apple after updates. Check: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → The Forge → make sure Body Mass is toggled on for both read and write.

**Bodyweight is duplicating.**
If you log inside the app *and* on a smart scale, both write to Health. The Forge reads Health and shows the most recent entry — but won't deduplicate scale entries. Pick one source of truth.

### Data & privacy

**Where is my data stored?**
Supabase (US-based, AWS-hosted). Full details: [Privacy Policy](https://theforgefitness.app/privacy).

**Can I export my data?**
Yes. Email support@theforgefitness.app from the address tied to your account and we'll send you a JSON export of everything within 7 days.

## Reporting a bug

The more specific, the faster we can fix it. Please include:

- iPhone model and iOS version
- App version (Settings → About → Version)
- What you were doing when it broke
- Screenshot or screen recording if you can grab one
